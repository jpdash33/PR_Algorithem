LINK ANALYSIS ALGORITHM

PageRank
Algorithm

A link-analysis algorithm that measures the relative importance of nodes in a directed network.

PageRank gives higher importance to nodes that receive links from other important nodes.

CORE IDEA

A page is important when important pages link to it.

PageRank does not simply count the number of links. It also considers the importance of the pages providing those links.
